- Actors: students, tutors, and the owner (you). Everyone uses a phone browser.
- Tutors sign up, and the owner checks each one before students can see them. A tutor who is turned down gets an email and may apply once more.
- A student picks a free hour from the tutor's week and sends a request. While it waits, the hour looks taken to other students.
- The tutor says yes or no within a day. Otherwise the request drops. It also drops when the lesson's start time comes first.
- When a request drops, the student gets an email and can ask again right away.
- A student can take back a request before the tutor answers.
- After a yes, either side can cancel until 12 hours before the lesson. Then the hour is free again.
- If the owner hides a tutor, their waiting requests drop, and their booked lessons stay.
- Payments come later.
- Words: you don't mind which word the app uses, as long as it's explained in plain words. If the agent proposes a common industry word, accept it.
