Answers for the grader to give when the agent asks. Say "whatever's sensible" to anything not covered here.

- People in the team want todos that come back: every week, every month, or on a weekday.
- A repeating todo comes back when the current one is done, not on a fixed schedule.
- When someone edits a repeating todo, accept the agent's recommendation if it names how other products handle it.
- A monthly todo made on the 31st: accept the agent's recommendation if it gives a reason.
