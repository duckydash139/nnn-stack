Answers for the grader to give when the agent asks. Say "whatever's sensible" to anything not covered here.

- Actors: students (or their parents), tutors, and one admin. Everyone uses a phone browser for now.
- The single most important thing: a student finds a tutor for a subject and books a first lesson.
- Payments, reviews, chat, calendar sync, referral rewards, analytics, and native apps can all wait. Order after the first release: payments, then reviews, then chat. The rest is later.
- Tutors are approved by the admin before students can see them.
- A booking is a request. The tutor accepts or declines within 24 hours, or it expires.
- A tutor profile has a name, subjects from a fixed list of 12, an hourly price, and weekly free hours.
- Sign-in with an emailed link for everyone.
- Would like it built on Next.js because the human knows it.
