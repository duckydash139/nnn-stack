Answers for the grader to give when the agent asks. Say "whatever's sensible" to anything not covered here.

- Fast means results appear within 300 ms while the list has up to 10,000 todos.
- Relevant means every word typed appears in the title, ignoring case. Order results with active todos first, then newest first.
- Elasticsearch: a colleague suggested it. The real need is the 300 ms limit.
- Search covers both the active and done lists.
- An empty search shows the normal lists.
